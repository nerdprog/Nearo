# Nearo 🌐

### Anonymous Confessions. Local Connections. Temporary Stories.

Nearo is a location-based anonymous confession website that lets people share their thoughts, confessions, and random moments with others nearby. Inspired by the fun of college confession pages, Nearo brings a local, temporary, and anonymous social experience to the web.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://nearo-rust.vercel.app

💻 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/nerdprog/Nearo

---

## 💡 The Idea Behind Nearo

The idea for Nearo came from seeing students in my college use Instagram confession pages and NGL links to share thoughts anonymously. I wanted to build something similar but with a different experience.

I combined the community-driven feel of Reddit with Snapchat's disappearing-content concept to create a platform focused on nearby people and temporary posts.

## ✨ Key Features

* **📍 Location-Based Access:** Posts are intended for people within a 2 km radius.
* **👤 Anonymous Posting:** Users can share their thoughts without displaying their identity publicly.
* **⏳ 24-Hour Posts:** Posts automatically disappear after 24 hours.
* **💬 Community Experience:** Discover confessions and thoughts shared by people nearby.
* **🌐 Live Deployment:** The website is hosted on Vercel and the source code is available on GitHub.

## 🎯 What Makes Nearo Different?

Unlike traditional social media platforms that focus on permanent profiles and a global audience, Nearo focuses on a nearby community and temporary conversations.

The combination of location-based access, anonymous sharing, and disappearing posts creates a different way for people to express themselves and connect with what's happening around them.

## 🛠️ Development

I was responsible for coming up with the idea, planning the features, and deciding how the platform should work. I used AI tools to assist with development and turn the concept into a working website.

I also pushed the source code to GitHub and deployed the project using Vercel, making it accessible online.
